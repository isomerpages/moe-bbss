<p><strong>Department Brief</strong>
</p>
<p>The Humanities Department embarks on a curriculum to develop in students
an inquisitive mind through inquiry-based pedagogy and to make learning
more real by providing authentic learning experiences. Throughout their
four to five years of education in our school we aim to instill in students
the need to care for the Earth, an appreciation of the past, culture and
heritage and to develop a sense of belonging to Singapore.</p>
